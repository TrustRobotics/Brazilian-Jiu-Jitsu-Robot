# BJJ Robot Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Closed-Loop Demonstration

Demonstrate a humanoid that can:

1. recognize five fundamental BJJ positions;
2. maintain a multimodal interaction state;
3. recognize several techniques;
4. choose context-appropriate actions;
5. respond to a human counter;
6. update its state and select a subsequent action;
7. recognize a tap and release safely; and
8. recognize when it has been caught and tap out.

## Future Work

- richer positional and technique ontologies;
- relational-state schemas;
- persona policy cards;
- adaptive training modes;
- world and physics model integration; and
- robot-versus-robot training.
